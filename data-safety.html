Data Safety

A plain-language overview of how GlycoLess may handle information, depending on the features and third-party services included in the production Android application.

Effective date: September 20, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 20, 2026

Important: Play Console is authoritative

This page is a supplemental, plain-language explanation of GlycoLess data handling. The official Google Play Data Safety declaration must always be completed and maintained according to the exact production Android build, SDKs, permissions, data flows, and Google Play requirements.

Before publishing a new production version, verify this page and the Google Play Data Safety form against the actual AAB and all enabled SDKs.

Data handling overview

G

Guest Mode

GlycoLess is designed so that normal guest tracking data can remain local on the user's device.

  • No normal Firebase account is required for local guest tracking.
  • Food logs and progress can remain stored locally.
  • Guest mode should not query a private user's Firestore data.
A

Signed-in Accounts

When a user signs in, supported application data may be stored locally and synchronized with cloud infrastructure.

  • Account information may be processed for authentication.
  • Food and progress information may synchronize with the user's account.
  • Cloud data is intended to be account-scoped.
F

Food & Nutrition Data

Information entered or saved through food tracking may include:

  • Food names
  • Serving quantities
  • Sugar and nutrition values
  • Daily goals and tracking information
  • Historical food logs
S

Scanner & Product Lookup

When scanning functionality is enabled, GlycoLess may process barcode information to look up supported food products.

  • Camera access may be required.
  • Barcode values may be sent to a product lookup service.
  • Product nutrition data may be returned from an external database.
P

Purchases & Premium

Premium access may depend on Google Play purchase or subscription information.

  • Purchase status may be checked to provide premium access.
  • Payment processing is handled by Google Play.
  • GlycoLess does not need to directly process your full payment card details for Google Play purchases.
T

Technical Information

Depending on the production SDK configuration, technical information may be processed for reliability, security, diagnostics, analytics, or crash reporting.

  • Device and application information
  • Technical error information
  • Performance or diagnostic information

How data can move through GlycoLess

1

Enter or scan

You enter food information or use an available scanner/product lookup feature.

2

Local processing

Supported information can be stored locally so normal tracking can remain responsive and available offline.

3

Cloud synchronization

For signed-in users, supported data may synchronize with cloud services when connectivity is available.

Security and access

GlycoLess is designed to use account-scoped access for cloud data and reasonable security controls. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their devices, authentication methods and account credentials secure.

Retention and deletion

Registered users can request account deletion. Local guest information may remain on the device until the user clears application data, removes the relevant information, or uninstalls the application.

Certain information may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.
