Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GlycoLess may collect, use, store, protect, and delete information when you use the GlycoLess Android application.

Important: GlycoLess is a food, nutrition, sugar-tracking, and educational application. It is not a medical diagnosis or treatment service.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the GlycoLess Android application and describes the information practices associated with the service operated by Sensora Labs.

By using GlycoLess, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, GlycoLess may process the following categories of information:

Category Examples Purpose
Account information Name, email address, authentication identifiers Account creation, authentication and account management
Food & nutrition data Food logs, sugar values, serving information, goals and tracking data Tracking, calculations, history and progress
Product information Barcode values and nutrition information Product lookup and food logging
Device information Technical information required for app operation Security, reliability, diagnostics and compatibility
Purchase information Subscription or purchase entitlement Premium feature access

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide and improve GlycoLess features, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Saving food logs and nutrition tracking information.
  • Calculating progress and tracking goals.
  • Providing barcode and food product lookup functionality.
  • Providing premium features and verifying purchase entitlements.
  • Maintaining application security, stability and reliability.
  • Responding to support requests.

4. Local and Cloud Storage

Guest mode

GlycoLess guest mode is designed to keep normal user tracking data locally on the device. Guest users should not require a Firebase account for normal local tracking.

Signed-in users

When you create or sign in to an account, supported application data may be stored locally and synchronized with cloud services used by GlycoLess, including Firebase/Cloud Firestore.

Local-first behavior

GlycoLess may use local-first storage so that normal tracking functionality can continue when an internet connection is unavailable. Synchronization may occur when connectivity becomes available.

5. Third-Party Services and Sharing

GlycoLess may use third-party services required to provide specific features. Depending on the production build and enabled features, these may include:

  • Firebase / Google services for authentication, cloud storage, application infrastructure and related services.
  • Google Play for application distribution and in-app purchases/subscriptions.
  • Open Food Facts for barcode/product information where the feature is enabled.
  • Advertising, analytics or crash reporting providers where such services are included in the production build.

We do not sell personal information as a commercial data product.

6. Device Permissions

Some GlycoLess features may request Android permissions when required. For example, barcode or food scanning may require camera access.

Permissions should be requested only when the related feature is used and should be used for the stated feature purpose.

Camera access is not intended to mean that GlycoLess continuously records camera activity.

7. Security

Sensora Labs uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission, cloud service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested service, maintain an account, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or maintain legitimate security and operational records.

Local guest data may remain on the device until the user removes the data, uninstalls the application, or otherwise clears the application's local storage.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Registered users may request deletion of their GlycoLess account and associated account data through the available account deletion process.

See the dedicated Account Deletion page for instructions.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or similar purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

GlycoLess is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
