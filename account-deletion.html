Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your GlycoLess account and associated account data using the process described below.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Delete your account from GlycoLess

If account deletion is available in your installed version of GlycoLess, the preferred method is to use the in-app account deletion option.

1

Open GlycoLess

Open the GlycoLess Android application and sign in to the account you want to delete.

2

Open Profile

Go to your Profile section and open Settings.

3

Open Data & Privacy

Find the Data & Privacy section inside Settings.

4

Select Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps shown by the application.

Request deletion by email

If the in-app deletion option is not available or you cannot access your account, you can contact Sensora Labs directly.

Account deletion email
sensoralabs@gmail.com

Suggested subject: GlycoLess Account Deletion Request

What to include in your request

To help us locate the correct account, provide enough information to identify the account, such as the email address associated with the GlycoLess account.

Never send your password, Google authentication codes, payment card numbers, or other sensitive authentication credentials by email.

What happens after a deletion request?

After a valid deletion request is received, account information and associated data will be reviewed for deletion or de-identification according to the applicable data practices and legal requirements.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Deletion of an account may not necessarily delete information held independently by third-party services under their own policies or legal obligations.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your GlycoLess account does not necessarily cancel an active Google Play subscription.

If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it through the applicable Google Play subscription controls.

Uninstalling the application also does not necessarily cancel an active subscription.

Guest mode data

GlycoLess guest mode is designed for local device-based tracking without requiring a normal cloud account.

Local guest information may remain on the device until the user clears the application's local storage, removes the relevant data, or uninstalls the application.

More information

For more information about how GlycoLess handles information, see the Privacy Policy and Data Safety pages.
